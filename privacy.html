Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026 | FluentWave Technologies
Core Commitment: FluentWave strictly collects student identification, academic records, and contact data solely to process admissions evaluations, assess scholarship eligibility, and facilitate verified university routing. We never sell student personal data to third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, take our Global Readiness Assessment, or upload application dossiers, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full legal name, date of birth, nationality, country of residence, WhatsApp phone number, and email address.
  • Academic & Evaluation Signals: Secondary school certificates (e.g. WAEC, Diplôme d'État, KCSE, Baccalauréat, Cambridge A-Levels), examination percentages, graduation year, and intended academic faculties.
  • Language & Proficiency Signals: Self-declared English and French language comfort scores and official test results if provided.
  • Identification & Verification Documents: Digital scans or photographs of government passports, academic marksheets, degree certificates, and passport-size photographs submitted for admissions screening.
  • Google Account Data: When signing in with Google OAuth, we receive your verified Google email address, profile name, and unique user identifier.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed to deliver governed educational mobility services:

  • Computing your bilingual Global Readiness Score (GRS) and evaluating eligibility for partner institutional scholarships.
  • Generating customized document verification checklists tailored to your target study destination.
  • Transmitting verified application packages to accredited partner universities (e.g., in India, Turkey, Cyprus, Russia, Mauritius, or Europe) upon your instruction.
  • Sending timely admissions milestone notifications, visa documentation guidance, and interview reminders via WhatsApp and email.
  • Maintaining an immutable, append-only audit trail (case_events) to protect your referral attribution and admissions integrity.

3. Document Security & Storage

We treat international identity documents with enterprise-grade security:

  • All sensitive scans (passports, national certificates) are encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) and at rest using AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Uploaded document URLs are accessed via time-limited, pre-signed cryptographic tokens that expire after 15 minutes.
  • Access to unredacted passport scans is strictly restricted to certified FluentWave admissions reviewers and authorized university registrar personnel.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We only share information under strict operational guidelines:

  • Accredited Partner Institutions: Upon receiving your approval, your structured candidate dossier is routed to the admissions offices of your selected institutions.
  • Government & Diplomatic Authorities: Where required to verify authentic visa invitation letters and embassy appointment packages.
  • Service Providers: Secure infrastructure providers (PostgreSQL/Supabase cloud storage, Google Identity Services, Twilio/WhatsApp notification gateways) bound by non-disclosure and strict data processing agreements.

5. Your Data Rights

Regardless of your country of residence, FluentWave affords you full control over your personal data:

  • Access & Correction: You may review and request corrections to your academic profile or contact information at any time via your student dashboard.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request the permanent deletion of your account and uploaded document scans once your admissions cycle is complete.
  • Withdrawal of Application: You may transition your application to WITHDRAWN at any stage prior to university matriculation.
