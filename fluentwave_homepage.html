Frequently Asked Questions

Everything prospective international students and families need to know about studying in India, visas, safety, and financing.

🌍 Are African and international high school diplomas recognized globally?
Yes, absolutely. Partner universities grant full international equivalence to national certificates, including West African WASSCE/WAEC, Central & East African Diplômes d'État, Baccalauréat, KCSE, and Cambridge A-Levels. FluentWave verifies and translates your qualification directly so you avoid unnecessary entrance exams.
🛡️ How safe are Indian universities for international students?
All our partner institutions operate gated campuses with 24/7 security, biometric hostel entry, and dedicated International Student Officers. There are thriving international and African student associations on campus to welcome and mentor new arrivals from day one.
💰 What is the true annual cost (Tuition + Food + Hostel)?
Studying in India is one of the most cost-effective global pathways. Top-tier accredited degrees (B.Tech, BBA, Pharmacy) range between $3,500 and $5,500 USD per year, which includes university tuition, campus hostel accommodation, and meals. Merit-based scholarships covering 20% to 50% of tuition are awarded to students with strong high school academic records.
🗣️ What if my English is basic? Will I struggle with lectures?
For students from non-English speaking or bilingual backgrounds (French, Portuguese, Arabic), Our partner universities offer structured English Bridge Programs (EAP) and intensive foundations before degree classes begin, ensuring you transition with confidence and graduate fully fluent.
🛂 How does the Student Visa & embassy process work in my country?
Once your FluentWave application is approved, the university issues an official Admission & Visa Invitation Letter. Our admissions team provides full document verification packages and guides you step-by-step through your local embassy appointment.
